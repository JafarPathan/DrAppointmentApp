## Appendix: FAQ for doctors
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Do my patients need the app? | For booking and the live queue, yes. Walk-ins can still be added by your staff with just a name and phone |
| Can my receptionist use it? | Yes, with a separate login and only the permissions you give. Staff can't see medical notes |
| What if the internet is down? | The dashboard shows the last synced list. Changes sync when you're back online |
| Who owns the data? | The patient owns their data. You're the treating doctor. We are the processor and never sell or share it |
| Is it legal under the DPDP Act? | It's designed for DPDP compliance: consent, encryption, access logs, deletion rights and breach procedures |
